Community standards

LIVED Yaps Rules

These rules apply to contributors participating in campaigns on LIVED Yaps.

01 · One accountUse your own account. No duplicate or account-farming.
02 · No spamAvoid repetitive, copy-pasted or meaningless posts and replies.
03 · No botsAutomated engagement, scripts and artificial activity are prohibited.
04 · No fake engagementDo not buy or manipulate likes, replies, followers or impressions.
05 · Quality mattersMeaningful engagement, relevance, consistency and useful interaction matter more than volume alone.
06 · Follow campaign rulesEach campaign may have additional requirements that must be followed.
07 · No misleading claimsDo not make false claims about a project, token, product, partnership or returns.
08 · Respect othersNo harassment, threats, impersonation, hate speech or abusive behaviour.
09 · Leaderboard reviewRankings may be reviewed before final rewards are confirmed.
10 · Reward eligibilityOnly contributors who meet the published campaign requirements are eligible.
Suspicious, fraudulent or manipulated activity may be excluded from scoring or rewards. Campaign-specific rules and reward terms are shown before participation.
